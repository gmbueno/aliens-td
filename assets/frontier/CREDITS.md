# Frontier sprites

Generated in the user's PixelForge studio on November 5, 2026:
https://codevon-com.github.io/pixelforge/

Three 4×4 sheets of native-alpha 64px sprites plus one opaque, tileable 64px
water texture. The user's original `assets/slots/weapons/handgun.png` supplied
the style reference for all three sheets: sculpted, worn industrial metal,
orthographic top view, muzzle pointing down. The six original starter-weapon
PNGs and original weapon/special card art remain in use.

Includes alternate weapon models, RPG/laser/flamethrower art, basalt/ice/growth/
cargo obstacles, cache states, props, and dedicated Mender, Juggernaut, Ravager
and Broodmother sprites. The studio marks generated exports **CC0-1.0**.

`provenance.json` records the complete prompts, model, sheet hashes, file mapping
and rounded costs. The studio reported **US$0.0604 total** for these four
generations. No credentials are included. Water ripples, haste arrows, beams,
flame cones and critical-hit feedback are rendered by the game.
