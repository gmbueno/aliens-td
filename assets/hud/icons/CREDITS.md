# Tactical HUD sprites

Created on November 5, 2026 with the user's PixelForge studio:
https://codevon-com.github.io/pixelforge/

Two 2×2 sheets, 64×64 pixels per sprite, native alpha transparency, original
colors. Exported directly as individual PNGs; no background removal needed.
The existing `assets/hud/lives-plaque.png` was supplied as a material reference.
The game also reuses the edges of the original life, credits and player plaques.

PixelForge marks these exports **CC0-1.0**. Generation metadata, prompts and sheet
hashes are in `provenance.json`. The studio reported US$0.0197 per sheet
(US$0.0394 total). The unused speed-chevron variation is kept out of the runtime.

The numbers, labels, progress tracks and controls remain live HTML/SVG for
legibility, responsiveness and accessibility. No credentials are included.
